Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. Create dishes with ingredients and servings and organize them with categories and favorites.

Does it create groceries from the menu?

Yes. The app builds a shopping preview from planned dishes and combines matching ingredients.

Can I account for food already at home?

Yes. Remove those items from the preview before adding groceries to your list.
