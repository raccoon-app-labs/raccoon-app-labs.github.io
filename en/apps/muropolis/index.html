Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

What can I do in Muropolis?

Build and develop a town, collect cat residents, place decorations and paint the landscape.

Can I pick up the cats?

Yes. The game lets you interact with cats and keep favorites on the Purr Shelf.

Can I design the map?

Yes. The landscape workshop lets you add paths, water and ground surfaces.
