Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

What do you study in Hanzi Bloom?

Chinese words and characters by HSK level: readings, meanings, components and review.

Can I break characters into parts?

Yes, where an analysis is available. Independent characters should not be artificially split; graphic components do not always explain origin or meaning.

Does it replace exam preparation?

It is a vocabulary and practice tool, not a guarantee of an HSK exam result.
