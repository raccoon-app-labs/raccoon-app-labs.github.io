Information used for your account and purchase

We use your email to send a short-lived sign-in code, verify your account, link a purchase to you and restore access. The service stores account identifiers, session and code verification records, order identifiers, product, amount, currency, accepted terms version, payment status and access expiry. Request information, including IP address, is used for rate limiting and service security. Support messages are used to resolve your request.

The checkout does not receive or store your full card number or card security code. You enter payment details on lava.top. The provider processes payment information under its own policies and sends payment status information to our server. Purchasing does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

Service providers and account region

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request data. Email delivery uses the configured mail service. lava.top processes payments. The international account API and its storage must be configured before this checkout opens; this page does not fall back to the Russian account API or the legacy Yandex function. The account region selected when creating your account remains its storage region; travel does not migrate an account.

The international flow is currently prepared for launch. Its deployed hosting, storage and backup providers and locations must be identified here before accepting international purchases. Until configured, checkout and sign-in on this page remain unavailable. This notice does not claim a particular international hosting location that has not been deployed.

Your browser and the apps

The checkout uses sessionStorage for your browser session, account email and pending order reference. This helps recover an interrupted payment without creating another invoice. Signing out removes this local session and requests server-side logout. Closing a session normally clears sessionStorage; browser restore settings may preserve tabs. Do not use a shared browser for a purchase unless you sign out afterwards.

Learning progress has its own in-app storage and account synchronization settings. Checkout does not send learning answers to the payment provider. Subscription expiry or a refund does not automatically delete learning progress.

Retention, security and requests

Codes and sessions are time-limited. Account and access records are used while needed to provide and restore the service. Payment and dispute records may be retained for applicable accounting and legal obligations. Access is restricted, connections use HTTPS, and secrets stay on the server; no security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Contact support to request access to, correction or deletion of account data. We verify account ownership and consider active purchases, refunds and required payment record retention before deletion. We do not ask you to send a card number or sign-in code. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete the server account. Additional rights and obligations under applicable law remain available.

A purchase must be made by a person capable of entering the contract, or by an appropriate legal representative. This checkout does not request a date of birth.
