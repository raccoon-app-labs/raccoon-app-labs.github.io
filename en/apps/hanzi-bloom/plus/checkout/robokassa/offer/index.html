Hanzi Plus: RUB purchases through Robokassa

English explanation for the existing RUB purchase account. Updated 17 November 2026.

Read the binding terms before paying.

The contract is the Russian RUB public offer, version 2026-10-26-web-1. This explanation describes that purchase flow for readers outside Russia. It does not introduce a separate international account or a USD tariff. The Russian offer governs the purchase; mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.

The account and the product

Hanzi Plus provides one shared paid term in Hanzi Bloom (com.enotessa.hanzi) and Hanzi Keys (com.enotessa.chinesekeys). Bloom includes its published HSK 1–3 lessons without lesson-unlock ads. Keys includes its protected lessons, practice and learning features without ads. HSK 4–6 marked as coming soon are not sold as already published lessons. These are digital app features; no physical delivery, personal tuition or exam result is promised.

Use the existing RUB account, labelled “Russia” or “Russian account” in some app versions. Choose that account in both apps and sign in with the same email used for this order. This is the purchase and storage account, independently of your citizenship, interface language or Google Play country. A separate global account does not receive this RUB purchase. Supported account data and cloud progress are processed by the existing Hanzi service in Russia.

Price, payment and availability

The existing RUB plans are one calendar month or 12 calendar months. The normal prices in the binding offer are RUB 299 and RUB 2,999 respectively. Review the current price, currency, selected term and any available discount on checkout before accepting and paying. The order and charge are in RUB. Your issuing bank may convert the amount and may charge its own fees; a foreign-currency amount is not guaranteed by Hanzi.

Payment uses Robokassa’s secure page. A card works only if Robokassa, its payment partners and the issuing bank support that particular transaction and the relevant restrictions permit it. Availability is not guaranteed for every card, bank or country. Enter card numbers, security codes and bank confirmations only on the payment service’s page. Hanzi does not receive your full card number or CVC.

This is a one-time payment for the selected term. There is no automatic renewal or recurring charge. A later extension is a new purchase at the price and terms shown at that time. The seller supplies the required professional-income-tax receipt; a payment-service confirmation does not replace that obligation.

Activation, outages and restoration

Install a current supported app version before purchase. Internet access is required to pay, activate, restore and sync. Plus activates after server confirmation of payment; creating an invoice or returning to a success page cannot activate it. The term begins at actual activation. One calendar month ends on the corresponding date in the following month, or its last day if that date does not exist. An extension of active Plus is added after the existing expiry.

The website checks the purchase service before opening payment. A connection can still fail after you have reached Robokassa, so a payment may succeed while confirmation is delayed. Do not pay again. Return to the website, sign in with the same email and use “Check my Plus”; use the same RUB account in either app. The server retains the order and retries payment verification and access recovery after a service interruption. Activation is due within 24 hours after payment confirmation under the binding offer. Contact support with the invoice number and date if access is missing.

Reinstalling or changing devices does not require another payment for the remaining term. Supported cloud progress is restored by the same account. Local changes that were never synced may be lost. Expiry of Plus does not itself delete your learning progress; account deletion ends cloud restoration.

Cancellation, refunds and support

You may withdraw from the service by emailing the seller; no personal explanation for withdrawal is required. Provide the account email, invoice number or other details sufficient to find the payment, and the payment date. Never send card details, CVC, bank codes or an email password.

Under section 6 of the binding offer, only documented actual expenses directly related to fulfilling this contract may be deducted to the extent permitted by applicable law. There is no fixed penalty, automatic bank-fee deduction or unconditional withholding of the entire payment. A duplicate payment, non-activation or service defect is considered separately with the remedies provided by law. If access was not activated and the seller has no documented expenses for that order, the payment is refunded in full.

The offer provides for considering the request and returning funds within 10 calendar days of receiving the request and information needed to identify the payment, unless law requires an earlier period. Refunds use the original payment method where the payment service supports it; the bank’s posting time depends on the bank. Full details, dispute procedures and mandatory rights are in the binding offer.
