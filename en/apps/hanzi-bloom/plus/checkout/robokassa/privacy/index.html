Information used by this checkout

Your email is used to send a sign-in code, find your purchase account, identify the order, deliver service messages and help restore Plus. A successful sign-in creates a limited-duration session. Email is not used for marketing through this checkout. The server uses hashed codes and session credentials, request limits and technical security records to help protect access.

Order records include the account identifier, invoice and request identifiers, selected app and term, RUB amount and currency, accepted terms version, payment status, confirmation and access dates, and information needed for refunds or disputes. A payment link may be retained to resume that same order. A separate recovery queue keeps the payment reference, merchant, RUB amount, hashed account binding and verification state so that a confirmed payment can be reconciled after an interruption. It is not a separate global account.

IP addresses and technical connection information are available to the infrastructure when you visit or call the service. They are used for delivery, security and limiting abuse. Support receives the email, message and files you choose to send. Do not send full card numbers, CVC, sign-in codes or bank confirmations to support.

Storage and service providers

The existing Hanzi RUB account, subscription, order and supported cloud-progress service runs through api1.hanzibloom.ru on the current server in Russia. International website requests use its /international gateway and the same RUB account records. Your interface language, citizenship or Google Play country does not move that account to another region. Account records are not published in the website’s source repository.

Robokassa receives the information needed to process the chosen payment, including the order reference, email, product, amount and currency, and returns payment status. Card and bank authentication information is entered directly with the payment service. Hanzi does not receive full card numbers, CVC or bank authentication codes. See Robokassa’s privacy policy.

The configured email service delivers sign-in emails, and support mail is handled in Google/Gmail as described in the existing policy. Public website pages are hosted on the website server, with GitHub Pages also serving the public fallback. These hosts receive normal technical visit information; the public GitHub repository does not store the account database. See the Google privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement. External services may process information outside Russia under their own policies. Russian account storage does not mean every provider operates only in Russia.

Browser storage and access recovery

This checkout uses sessionStorage for the account session, email and saved order references, including the request identifier needed to avoid creating a second invoice after an uncertain result. It does not store card details there. The language picker may retain your language preference in localStorage. Signing out removes the checkout’s local session; clearing site data also removes these local records. Browser session storage is separate from the server’s payment record, so clearing it does not cancel an order or delete the account.

No advertising or analytics counter is installed in this checkout. External payment pages and public hosting infrastructure have their own technical storage rules. The app’s advertising, analytics, device storage and cloud-progress processing are described separately in the Russian policy.

Purpose, retention and your rights

Account and order data are processed to take steps you request, fulfil the purchase, protect the service, restore access and handle refunds. Accounting records are also retained where required by law. Publishing this notice is not consent to marketing or unrestricted data transfers. Where separate consent is necessary, it must be obtained separately.

Account and supported cloud-progress records are not automatically deleted when Plus expires. Purchase, recovery and support records are kept for the period needed to fulfil obligations, resolve failures, refunds and disputes, and meet required record-keeping duties; they are then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Some required payment records may remain after account deletion. Temporary sign-in and security records expire under the existing service’s retention rules.

You can request access to information about your data, correction, restriction or deletion, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email the operator from your account email with the subject “Delete Hanzi account” if you want the server account removed. Ownership is checked to protect your account. Deletion includes supported cloud progress and ends that account’s ability to restore Plus; if you have an active purchase, you can separately discuss withdrawal and a refund under the purchase terms. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority or court. Applicable mandatory rights and time limits remain in force.
