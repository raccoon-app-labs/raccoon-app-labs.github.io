Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. Start with core radicals, then move to related characters and review.

What does “214 radicals” mean?

It is a reference to the Kangxi radical system. A radical helps classify and look up characters; a character’s meaning is not always the sum of its parts.

How is it different from Hanzi Bloom?

Hanzi Keys focuses on radicals, shapes and practice. Hanzi Bloom focuses on vocabulary by HSK level.
