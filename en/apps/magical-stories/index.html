Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can I record a story in my own voice?

Yes. You can create your own narration, including a mom’s or dad’s voice.

What can I do besides reading?

Listen, record family narrations, save favorites, resume reading and find objects in story illustrations.

Is there a child mode?

Yes. The app includes a separate child mode with a protected exit.
