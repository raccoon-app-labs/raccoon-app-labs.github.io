Before you begin

Good questions. Clear answers.

How do the controls work?

Tap on a wall to jump. One tap in the air redirects the jump; holding adds thrust while charge remains.

What makes the walls different?

The game includes slippery, charged, brittle and magnetic surfaces, among others.

Are there daily challenges?

Yes. There is a daily route, missions and separate daily run results.
