Cancellation and Refunds

Version dated November 16, 2026 · recurring-2026-11-16

Disabling auto-renewal stops future charges. Paid access remains until period expiry. Termination of service and refund of amounts already paid are handled separately under applicable law.

Disabling renewal and refusing payment-data use

Open Profile → Premium → Turn off renewal and wait for confirmation. No new charge requests are created and the payment reference is removed from the subscription. Reconnection requires new explicit consent.

You may also refuse further use of payment details or terminate service by emailing raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru from the account address. After receiving refusal, the Provider does not use previously supplied details for new periodic charges. Card numbers and bank codes are not required.

A request sent to the bank before cancellation may still be processing. This does not permit new charges or remove the right to dispute the transaction or seek a refund. A one-time month and story purchase create no recurring charges.

Termination and refunds

The user may terminate service and claim a refund in the cases and manner provided by Russian law, including for an unused period where legally applicable. Merely disabling future renewal does not replace a request to terminate current service and obtain a refund.

Grounds include failure to provide access, duplicate charges, charges after refusal of payment-data use, and other statutory grounds. Requests take into account services actually supplied and mandatory consumer rights. There is no blanket no-refund rule.

Submitting a request

Email raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru from the purchase address, or state that address. Include the story or Premium, date and amount, any payment ID, reason, and desired action: disabling renewal, service termination, or refund. A status screenshot can be provided if needed.

Never send a full card number, expiry date, CVC/CVV, passwords, SMS codes, or push-confirmation codes.

Review and payment

The Provider responds to and fulfils valid claims within applicable legal deadlines. Monetary claims subject to a ten-day deadline are fulfilled no later than 10 calendar days. Additional details are requested only as necessary to identify the operation and assess the claim.

Refunds go through the payment service to the original payment method. Crediting time depends on the bank and payment network. Access attributable to a refunded payment may be revoked; other separately paid access is unaffected.

Unknown status

If money was charged but access is missing or a payment is under review, do not pay again until resolved. The Provider checks the same operation with the payment service. Keep the ID and bank notice without disclosing payment details.
