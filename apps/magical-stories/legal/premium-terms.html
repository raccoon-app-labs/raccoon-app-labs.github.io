Account means the user record identified by a verified email address and used to grant and restore purchased access.

Premium means paid access to the entire available story library and ad-free use of the App during the paid period.

3. Service

The Provider grants the user remote access to the selected digital content and App features, and the user pays for the selected service.

One story — RUB 29

Access to the selected story without a time limit imposed by the Provider and without recurring charges.

Premium — RUB 199/month

Access to the entire available library without ads for one calendar month. Auto-renewal applies only when expressly offered and separately accepted.

Access is granted after the Provider receives reliable confirmation of successful payment from the payment service. Returning from the payment page does not itself confirm payment.

The purchase is linked to the email address supplied and verified by the user. To restore access on another device, the user must sign in with the same address. The user is responsible for the address being correct and for retaining access to that mailbox.

If access does not appear within a reasonable time after a charge, do not pay again. Contact support and provide the email address, date, amount, and any available payment identifier.

An internet connection may be required to operate the App, download content initially, verify a purchase, and restore access. Previously downloaded content may remain available offline subject to the App's technical capabilities and the current paid period.

5. Premium and recurring payments

5.1. Enabling renewal and consent

Auto-renewal applies only when expressly offered at checkout. The user sees the first amount, regular renewal amount, frequency, and cancellation method. To enable it, an adult selects the initially unchecked box “I agree to automatic charges under the offer terms” and confirms the first payment. A clickable Offer link is available before payment.

Consent history includes the account, confirmation time, Offer version, consent text and version, agreed first and regular amounts, and payment provider. Auto-renewal is not enabled without separate consent. A previous one-time month is not automatically converted into a recurring subscription.

Recurring payments are processed by Robokassa using a card issued by a Russian bank; foreign cards are unavailable for recurring payments. The Provider does not receive a full card number or CVC/CVV: a protected reference to the initial payment is used.

5.2. Price, period, date, and time

The base price is RUB 199 per calendar month. If the 20% first-purchase discount is available and used for Premium, the first month costs RUB 159.20, then RUB 199. The discount is used by one first successful purchase, a story or Premium. The final first and regular amounts are shown before consent; the discounted amount does not become the renewal price.

The first period starts at confirmed payment. Each subsequent period is one calendar month from the previous period’s end. Calculation uses UTC and the same time of day; if the corresponding day does not exist, the last day of that month is used. The next period is calculated from that adjusted date: for example January 31 → February 28 → March 28 (February 29 → March 29 in a leap year).

Premium management displays the next charge amount, date, and time in Moscow time (MSK, UTC+3). A request is sent no earlier than that moment in the next processing run, normally every 15 minutes. Bank or payment-service processing may take additional time. The App need not be opened for renewal.

An email states the date, amount, and cancellation method three days before planned renewal. No charge is initiated without a successfully sent notice or less than 24 hours after it is sent. A delayed notice can delay the actual request. No automatic charge is initiated after a processing interruption lasting more than seven days after period expiry.

A new period is granted only after confirmed payment. A confirmed bank decline disables auto-renewal; the user must initiate a new purchase. An unknown result is checked against the same operation: do not pay again until resolved. Premium access is suspended if the paid period has ended without a confirmed new payment.

5.3. Disabling renewal and refusing payment-data use

Open Profile → Premium → Turn off renewal. After confirmation no new charge requests are created, and the saved payment reference is removed from the subscription. Paid access remains until the period ends. Reconnection requires new explicit consent.

You may also refuse further use of payment details by emailing raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru from the account address. No card number or bank codes are required. After receiving refusal, the Provider does not use previously supplied details for new periodic charges.

A request sent to the bank before cancellation may still be processing; the App shows a pending operation. This does not permit new charges after refusal or restrict the right to dispute a payment or request a statutory refund.

5.4. Price changes

The regular renewal price is fixed in the consent. A website price change or price for new buyers does not itself increase existing automatic charges. An increase requires advance notice and new explicit consent to the new amount; no charge at the higher price is made before that consent. Paid periods keep their original terms. The user may decline and disable renewal.

See the Subscription Terms for a concise explanation.
