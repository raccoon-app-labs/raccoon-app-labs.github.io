Subscription management

Your Premium

Open the App to view status, the paid period, the next charge amount, date, and time in Moscow time (MSK, UTC+3), payment history, and disable auto-renewal. A one-time month has no future automatic charges.

Manage subscription on the website Manage in the AppContact support

How to disable auto-renewal

Profile → Premium → Turn off renewal. Wait for confirmation: no new charge requests are created; paid access remains until expiry. Reconnection requires new consent.

If the App is unavailable, email raccoon.labs.app@yandex.ru from the account address to refuse further payment-data use. Do not send card details. A request already sent to the bank may still be processing; status and refunds are checked separately.

If the App did not open

Start Magic Stories manually and go to Profile → Premium. On a new device, sign in with the same email address—you do not need to pay again for an active Premium period.
